About

Personalization with LLM memory

Turning users' activity across search, shopping, short-form video and live streaming into an LLM-summarized personal memory, and using it to retrieve content people actually want in their feed.

Training & agentic LLMs

SFT across the full cycle — data synthesis and filtering, recipes, evaluation, error analysis — plus agent models that reason over streaming, stateful sensor inputs.

Culture in NLP

Datasets and benchmarks showing how hate-speech judgments, social bias, and everyday knowledge differ across cultures and languages — and where LLMs fall short.

How I work

Evidence first: offline harnesses, log replay and simulations before A/B tests, careful separation of real effects from confounds, and plain statements of what the data cannot show.

Machine Learning Engineer · Agentic App AI · Seoul
